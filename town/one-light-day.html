The wave of light

17 Nov 2026: phones, flashlights and lit landmarks, raised together toward a point 16 billion miles away. Each beam left the instant it was sent; each arrived a day later. An unprompted greeting aimed at something with no duty to answer — the longest-distance hello humans have sent.
