# Question-index stamp checks

**This grades the date, not the answer under it.** A bumped line makes a quietly wrong passage pass; a `touch` fools the clock. Read the green mark as clock agreement only, and the number beside it as reads of one clock, not separate verdicts.

Derived from `stamp-verdicts.jsonl` on every sync. Consecutive identical verdicts are collapsed into one dated line whose count names its unit, so quiet PASSes cannot bury a FAIL or pose as certificates. This records a clock agreement between the index's typed stamp and the file's mtime; it is not a verdict about a person, a reader, or the town. The checker catches an edit that forgot to bump the stamp, not a deliberate `touch`.
